# Cognitive Simulator: Resilience & Adaptation

An interactive web-based simulator that models how individuals of different ages respond to stress, adversity, and life-changing decisions—grounded in research on resilience, emotion regulation, and cognitive science.

## About the Project

This simulator explores how people adapt to stressful life experiences. It features three characters at different life stages:

- **Samantha (16)** — navigating identity, peer pressure, and family conflict.
- **Fabian (27)** — balancing career, relationships, and purpose.
- **Mariana (48)** — facing aging, loss, and reinvention.

Each character responds to adversity through a system of states and endings shaped by their decisions. The project combines programming, psychology, and neuroscience to model resilience as a dynamic process—not a fixed trait.

The project began in December 2025 as a mod concept for *The Sims 4* and evolved into a standalone web-based simulator built from scratch with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
