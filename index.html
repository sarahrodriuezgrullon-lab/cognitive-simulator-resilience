Learning Journal

This project began in December 2025 as a mod concept for The Sims 4. I wanted to model how trauma affects Sims, but I realized the game didn't give me the control I needed to measure psychological variables. So I decided to build my own simulator from scratch.

I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript using CS50 from Harvard and The Human Mind from MIT. Every decision in this simulator is backed by research on resilience and emotion regulation. I learned that resilience is not about avoiding pain, but about choosing how to go through it.
